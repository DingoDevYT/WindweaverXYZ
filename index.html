About Windweaver

You are Jonathan, a Windweaver enforcer patrolling the skies - until a mysterious cannon blast shatters your flight attunement and sends you crashing to the ground. Stripped of your ability to fly, you must rely on the faint power still flickering in your scarf to make the long journey home to the Isle of Wind.

This high-speed action platformer emphasizes momentum-based movement and strategic boost management. Master the Wind-Dash to blast forward at incredible speeds, then recharge it mid-air using Fall-Charge. Chain dashes together, navigate obstacles at breakneck velocity, and discover the truth behind your fall as you race through vibrant environments filled with secrets, NPCs, and challenges. Every dash counts - speed is your weapon, and precision is your key to survival.

Core Movement System

Master two essential abilities to navigate the world at high speed and chain them together for maximum velocity.

Wind-Dash

Your primary boost ability. Blast forward in a burst of wind, covering massive distances instantly. Strategic use of Wind-Dash is key to maintaining speed and navigating challenging terrain. Each dash consumes your boost meter, so timing is everything.

Fall-Charge

Recharge your boost meter while airborne by channeling the wind during your descent. The longer you fall-charge, the more boost you recover. Balance risk and reward as you decide when to recharge and when to unleash another dash.

Game Features

Boost-Based Movement

Master the Wind-Dash and Fall-Charge system to maintain momentum and navigate levels at exhilarating speeds.

Strategic Boost Management

Balance aggressive dashing with smart recharging. Every decision affects your speed and ability to overcome obstacles.

Environmental Interaction

Navigate wind tunnels, dash through air rings, bounce off flowers, and use environmental elements to maintain flow.

Customizable Pins

Craft powerful pins at the blacksmith to modify your abilities and playstyle. Choose enhancements that match your strategy - whether it's faster recharge, extended dashes, or unique mechanics that transform how you move.

Story-Driven Adventure

Uncover the mystery behind your fall as you meet colorful NPCs and discover the truth about the Windweavers and the world below.

Multiplayer Races

Challenge friends to discover who is the fastest Windweaver. Compete in time trials and races across all levels.
